What we collect

MakarnaGo does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress (level percentages, selected costume and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Share cards

Images generated when you die or finish a level are created on your device. If you choose to share one, your device's own share sheet is used; where it goes is up to you.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from IP), app usage data and diagnostics to show and measure ads. If you don't grant tracking permission on iOS, you won't see personalized ads. Learn more about Google's data use at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Users in the EEA and UK are asked for consent, which can be changed at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

All game data lives only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset Progress inside the app, erases it.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
