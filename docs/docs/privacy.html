Frequently asked questions

How do I control the noodle?
Drag your finger up and down on the screen. In Settings you can switch to tap-and-hold controls instead.

How do I unlock the secret level?
It unlocks after 100 deaths across any levels. Check the lock icon on the home screen for your progress.

I lost my progress / want to reset it.
Progress is stored only on your device. Go to Settings → Reset Progress to start over.

Does the app track me?
See our Privacy Policy for details.

Languages

The app is available in Turkish, English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Russian, and follows your device's language automatically.
